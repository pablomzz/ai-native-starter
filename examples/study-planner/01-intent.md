# Intent: weekly minutes per subject

Author: Sam
Date: 251102

## Problem
I study for five subjects, but I only remember the ones I like. Before the exam I find out I barely touched chemistry. I have no quick way to see where my time went this week.

## Outcome
I type each study session (subject and minutes), and the page shows the total minutes per subject for this week, biggest first.

## Who and what it touches
- Users: me, and maybe two friends from class.
- Screens or features: one page with a form and a list.
- Data: subject names and minutes, saved in the browser only.

## Constraints
- No account and no server: everything stays on my laptop.
- Works on my phone screen too.

## Open questions
- Does the week start on Monday or Sunday? (Sam: Monday.)

## Done when
- [ ] I can add a session in under ten seconds.
- [ ] The totals update right away and are sorted biggest first.
- [ ] Closing and reopening the page keeps my sessions.
